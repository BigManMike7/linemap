LineMap Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

The short version

  • No accounts. No names, emails or phone numbers.
  • Your location is only used when you report a wait, and only after you allow it.
  • We keep where you started and stopped timing for 180 days, then delete it automatically.
  • No ads, no tracking, and we never sell or share your data.
  • You can delete your reports at any time in the app.

What LineMap is

LineMap is a free, non-profit iPhone app that shows how long the line is at the door of bars in State College, Pennsylvania. The wait times come from people using the app, who time the line while they stand in it.

What we collect

A random ID for your copy of the app. When you first open LineMap, it gets a random ID from our database provider, Supabase. It isn't linked to your name, email, phone number or Apple ID. Deleting the app loses the ID for good.

Your wait reports. When you time a line or tap "No wait", we save which bar it was, when you started, when you got in, how long you waited, and which bar the app suggested. We also save whether the report counts toward the estimate. For example, very short or very long timers don't count.

Your location, only when you report. When you start and stop a timer, or tap "No wait", the app sends your location and how accurate it was. We use it to check that you were actually at the bar, so the wait times stay trustworthy. We also save the distance between where you started and the bar's door.

What stays on your phone. While a timer is running, the app follows your location on your phone to work out when you got inside. iOS shows the blue location indicator while this happens. This location trail never leaves your phone. The app also uses your location on your phone to find the nearest bar and to show distances. That isn't sent anywhere.

You can browse the map and the list without ever allowing location.

What we don't do

  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking tools, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't sell, rent or share your data with anyone.
  • Other people using LineMap never see your individual reports or your location. They only see a combined wait estimate for each bar, such as "5-15 min".

How long we keep it

  • Start and stop locations: deleted automatically 180 days after we receive them.
  • Wait reports (bar, times, how long you waited): kept while LineMap runs, so we can show typical waits. They are tied only to your copy of the app's random ID, never to you personally.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Delete my data. This removes every report made from your copy of the app, including their locations.

If you delete the app first, the random ID is gone. Because your reports were never linked to you, we then have no way to tell which reports were yours. So use Delete my data before you delete the app.

To stop sharing your location, turn off Location Services for LineMap in your iPhone's Settings. You can still browse, but your reports won't count toward the estimate.

Services we rely on

  • Supabase stores our database, in the United States. Like any internet service, it sees your IP address when the app connects and keeps it in short-lived technical logs for security. We don't save your IP address with your reports. Supabase privacy policy.
  • Apple delivers the beta through TestFlight. Apple may share limited tester information with us, along with crash reports and any feedback you choose to send. Apple privacy policy.

Children

LineMap is about bar lines and isn't meant for children. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Changes to this policy

If LineMap starts using your data in a new way, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the app. For bigger changes, we'll also tell you in the app.
